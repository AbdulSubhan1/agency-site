# Engineering highlights

What this project does beyond "it works", and why.

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Where                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **One codebase, two brands, chosen at build time.** Each Vercel project sets `BRAND`; an unknown value fails the build.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Both brands share every page, form and fix, while each keeps its own tone, prices and look. Choosing at build time (not by hostname) keeps every page static and makes a wrong deploy fail loudly instead of showing the other brand. | `src/lib/brand.ts`, `brand.test.ts`                                                                           |
| **No database on the website.**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Leads belong in the CRM. The site holds no customer data and no database credentials, so a site breach exposes nothing.                                                                                                               | `AGENTS.md`                                                                                                   |
| **Content is typed data with a per-brand override layer.** Nine services and five products live once in `src/content/`; each brand file sets only what differs: positioning, tone, hero and CTAs, the featured order per region, industries, currency, time zone, spelling, service wording and theme.                                                                                                                                         | Both brands sell the same work, so one fix to a service updates both sites. A brand can't drift into a half-copied page, and adding a region (Saudi Arabia later) is one entry in a brand file.                                       | `src/content/`, `src/brands/`, `src/content/resolve.ts`                                                       |
| **The tests guard the content.** Every service is checked against a zod schema and must have a price, or an explicit "not offered", for every region. A brand may only feature services priced in that region, and may only show products whose follow-up service is offered there.                                                                                                                                                            | Content changes more often than code and is edited by non-developers. A missing price or a featured service with no price fails `npm test` instead of showing "undefined" on a live page.                                             | `src/content/content.test.ts`                                                                                 |
| **One spelling source.** Shared copy is written in US spelling; the UK brand swaps a short word list at render time, and a test fails if UK spelling slips into the shared copy.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Two copies of every sentence would drift. A short list plus a test keeps one source and correct spelling for each market.                                                                                                             | `src/lib/spelling.ts`                                                                                         |
| **Prices are numbers, formatted per brand.** Ranges are stored as whole numbers in the region's currency and formatted with `Intl` (`$2,500–5,000`, `AED 3,500–9,000`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Prices can be compared, sorted and checked by tests, and a currency's format is never typed by hand.                                                                                                                                  | `src/lib/money.ts`                                                                                            |
| **Motion with GSAP, one module.** ScrollTrigger and SplitText (free since GSAP 3.13) come from `src/lib/gsap.ts`, and every section animates inside `useGSAP` with `gsap.matchMedia`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | `useGSAP` cleans up on unmount, so pinned sections don't leak between pages. `matchMedia` makes "reduce motion" a first-class state: with it on, every section renders final and readable.                                            | `src/lib/gsap.ts`, `src/components/home/`                                                                     |
| **Scroll stories are data, not code.** The hero graph, the evening feed and the order story live in each brand's home content (`src/content/home-*.ts`, types in `examples.ts`); the components only animate them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | A story can move to another brand or page by moving data, and every example sits next to the copy that labels it.                                                                                                                     | `src/content/examples.ts`, `src/content/home-*.ts`                                                            |
| **Pin only where it fits.** Pinned, scrubbed sections run from tablet width up; on phones the same timeline plays once when it scrolls into view.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Pinning content taller than the screen hides its end (the fourth order step on a phone).                                                                                                                                              | `story-day.tsx`, `story-flow.tsx`, `services-rail.tsx`                                                        |
| **Brand colours as CSS variables.** The root layout writes the brand's theme to `<html>`, and Tailwind's `@theme inline` maps them to classes (`bg-night`, `text-accent`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | One component set, two looks, no per-brand CSS files; the theme lives next to the rest of the brand's settings.                                                                                                                       | `src/app/layout.tsx`, `src/app/globals.css`                                                                   |
| **Examples are labelled.** Every worked example says "Example" and the footer says they are illustrations.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | No client results exist yet, and the rule is that every number shown comes from a real project.                                                                                                                                       | `src/brands/*.ts`, `src/app/page.tsx`                                                                         |
| **Enquiries are signed server to server.** The form posts to a server action, which checks it again and signs the call to AgencyDesk with HMAC-SHA256 over the timestamp, method, path and body. A test pins the CRM's fixed signature vector.                                                                                                                                                                                                 | The secret never reaches the browser, a copied request expires after 5 minutes, and if either side changes how it signs, both test suites fail.                                                                                       | `src/lib/signature.ts`, `src/lib/agencydesk.ts`, `src/app/contact/actions.ts`                                 |
| **One schema for the browser and the server.** The same zod rules check the form before it's sent and again in the server action; the CRM's answer for a bad field shows under that field too.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | The visitor sees the same message whichever side catches the problem, and a hand-made request can't skip the checks.                                                                                                                  | `src/lib/lead-form.ts`                                                                                        |
| **Each brand asks in its own way.** US: two steps (what you need, then who you are) and a work email. Asia: WhatsApp number first with the opt-in tick, and the calling code decides UAE or Pakistan.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | US buyers expect email and a booked call; in the UAE and Pakistan WhatsApp is the main channel, and WhatsApp's policy needs an opt-in.                                                                                                | `src/components/contact/lead-form.tsx`                                                                        |
| **The form is sent by hand, not through the form's `action` prop.**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | React resets a form after its action, which would wipe what the visitor typed whenever the server sends a field back to fix.                                                                                                          | `src/components/contact/lead-form.tsx`                                                                        |
| **The contact form renders inside Suspense.**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | `cacheComponents` prerenders every page; the query (`?service=`, `?product=`) is only known per request, so only the form waits for it and the rest of the page stays static.                                                         | `src/app/contact/page.tsx`                                                                                    |
| **Every CRM call is reported to Pulse** (route, status, time, never the body), and the beacon loads once the site is registered there.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | A failing form shows up in monitoring before a visitor complains.                                                                                                                                                                     | `src/lib/pulse.ts`, `src/app/layout.tsx`                                                                      |
| **Every page names its own canonical address.**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Both brands share some service text, and the contact page is reached with `?service=` links; a canonical per page tells search engines which address to index instead of splitting rank across copies.                                | `alternates.canonical` in each page's metadata                                                                |
| **Structured data is built from the brand and content files** (Organization and WebSite on the home page, Service and breadcrumbs on each service page), with `<` escaped.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | It can never disagree with what the page says, a new service gets it for free, and content text can't break out of the script tag.                                                                                                    | `src/lib/structured-data.ts`, `src/components/site/json-ld.tsx`                                               |
| **Each brand uses its own logo files, cut from the owner's artwork** (white on transparent, in `public/brand/<id>/`): the full logo in the header and footer, the symbol on a dark tile for the favicon, home-screen icon and structured-data logo. `next/og` gets them as data URLs.                                                                                                                                                          | The masterplan says to keep the real logos' exact shapes, so nothing is redrawn. Sizes live in one map, and the share image and icons read the same files the header shows.                                                           | `src/components/site/brand-mark.tsx`, `src/lib/brand-assets.ts`, `src/app/icon.tsx`, `src/app/apple-icon.tsx` |
| **Each brand has its own type pair and palette** (DevXonic: Space Grotesk + Inter, electric blue; Radian Zest: Sora + Manrope, purple and magenta), set in the brand's `theme`. The accent stays light enough for dark text on buttons.                                                                                                                                                                                                        | The two brands should never look like one template recoloured, and every colour still comes from one place per brand.                                                                                                                 | `src/brands/*.ts`, `src/app/layout.tsx`                                                                       |
| **Header menus close by remembering the page they opened on.** The Services panel and the phone menu store the path they were opened at; on any other path they count as closed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Navigating closes them without an effect that sets state after render, so there is no flash of an open menu on the next page.                                                                                                         | `src/components/site/site-header.tsx`                                                                         |
| **The phone menu behaves like a dialog:** full screen, focus starts inside and wraps with Tab, Escape closes it and returns focus to the Menu button, and the page behind stops scrolling. The Services panel closes on Escape or a click outside.                                                                                                                                                                                             | Keyboard and screen-reader users never end up behind an open menu, as the masterplan's component contracts ask.                                                                                                                       | `src/components/site/site-header.tsx`                                                                         |
| **One closing call to action in the footer, skipped where a page already ends with one** (home, About, Contact, each service). Its wording lives in each brand file.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Every page ends with a next step, without two "start a project" blocks stacked on the same page.                                                                                                                                      | `src/components/site/footer-cta.tsx`, `src/brands/*.ts`                                                       |
| **Each brand has its own home page.** `page.tsx` picks `DevxonicHome` or `RadianzestHome`; both are built from shared section components and their own content file.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | The masterplan makes the two brands different businesses with different home pages; shared sections keep them cheap to maintain and consistent in quality.                                                                            | `src/app/page.tsx`, `src/components/home/*-home.tsx`, `src/components/home/sections.tsx`                      |
| **Nothing on the home pages claims proof we can't show.** Sections that need real projects, reviews or articles (work, testimonials, insights) are left out until they exist; DevXonic's tool list names only tools used in shipped code; Radian Zest's search section says plainly that nobody can guarantee rankings or AI citations.                                                                                                        | The masterplan forbids invented clients, metrics, partnerships and guarantees; an empty section is better than a made-up one.                                                                                                         | `src/content/home-*.ts`                                                                                       |
| **The FAQ uses native `<details>`.**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Keyboard and screen readers work without JavaScript, and every answer is in the HTML for search engines.                                                                                                                              | `src/components/home/sections.tsx`                                                                            |
| **Radian Zest opens with type, not a diagram.** The headline's sentences step in from the left, one per line, each rising out of its own mask; indents are sized so every line stays whole from tablet up.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | The masterplan asks for an editorial, type-led hero for the growth brand, clearly different from DevXonic's system diagram.                                                                                                           | `src/components/home/editorial-hero.tsx`                                                                      |
| **Motion follows Emil Kowalski's rules** (audit with his public skills, 2026-11-19): no scroll reveals on content, no endless loops, nothing grows from `scale(0)`, exits ease out, interface transitions name their properties and stay at 150–200ms on one strong ease-out token (`ease-ui`), buttons scale to 0.97 when pressed, hover only changes colour. The story, process line and hero entrances stay because they explain something. | Motion people see on every visit has to earn its place; fewer, faster, purposeful animations make the sites feel quicker and keep content readable for fast scrollers.                                                                | `src/app/globals.css`, `src/components/site/cta-link.tsx`, `src/components/home/*`                            |
| **Phones get the brand's dark colour in the status bar and no grey tap flash.**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | The status bar runs into the dark header, and buttons give their own press feedback instead of the browser's grey box.                                                                                                                | `generateViewport` in `src/app/layout.tsx`, `src/app/globals.css`                                             |
